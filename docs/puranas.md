# Mahāpurāṇas

### Canonical Eighteen Mahāpurāṇas

| S.No. | Mahāpurāṇa | Verses | Traditional Guna | Central Deity | Primary Focus & Subject Matter |
|:---:|:---|:---:|:---|:---|:---|
| 1 | **Brahma Purāṇa** *(Ādi Purāṇa)* | 10,000 | Rajas | Brahma | 245 chapters; holy places of Odisha; mythology, theories of war, temple art, and topics covering Vishnu and Shiva. |
| 2 | **Padma Purāṇa** | 55,000 | Sattva | Vishnu | Cosmology, nature of life, rivers, geography from northwest India to Tripura; avatars of Vishnu; pilgrimage centers. |
| 3 | **Viṣṇu Purāṇa** | 23,000 | Sattva | Vishnu | Vaishnava theology, dynastic genealogies, philosophical discourses, and avatar legends. |
| 4 | **Śiva Purāṇa** | 24,000 | Tamas | Shiva | Shaiva corpus; life and legends of Shiva and Parvati; originally described as having 100,000 verses in twelve samhitas abridged by Vyasa. |
| 5 | **Bhāgavata Purāṇa** | 18,000 | Sattva | Vishnu | Life and avatars of Vishnu (particularly Krishna), Bhakti philosophy, and dynastic genealogies. |
| 6 | **Nārada Purāṇa** *(Nāradīya Purāṇa)* | 25,000 | Sattva | Vishnu | Four Vedas, six Vedangas, astronomy, soteriology, and chapter summaries of the other 17 Mahapuranas. |
| 7 | **Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa** | 9,000 | Rajas | Brahma | Western India and Vindhya region; contains the *Devī Māhātmyam*, discussions on dharma, and the Mahabharata. |
| 8 | **Agni Purāṇa** | 15,400 | Tamas | Shiva / Agni | Encyclopedic work covering politics, law, grammar, Vastu Shastra, medicine, gemology, and warfare. |
| 9 | **Bhaviṣya Purāṇa** | 14,500 | Rajas | Brahma / Surya | Prophecies, rituals, cosmology, and sections aligned with ancient solar traditions. |
| 10 | **Brahmavaivarta Purāṇa** | 18,000 | Rajas | Brahma / Krishna | Centers on Radha and Krishna as supreme reality; legends of Brahma-Varaha and geography of major river basins. |
| 11 | **Liṅga Purāṇa** | 11,000 | Tamas | Shiva | Nature and symbolism of the Lingam, cosmology of Shaivism, and the Agni Lingam dispute resolution. |
| 12 | **Varāha Purāṇa** | 24,000 | Sattva | Vishnu | Focused on Narayana's Varaha avatar; contains pilgrimage guides (Mahatmyas) for Mathura and Nepal. |
| 13 | **Skanda Purāṇa** | 81,100 | Tamas | Shiva / Skanda | Longest Purana; birth of Skanda; exhaustive geographic and pilgrimage guide to sacred sites across India. |
| 14 | **Vāmana Purāṇa** | 10,000 | Rajas | Brahma / Vishnu | Focuses on North India and the Himalayan foothills region; contains Vaishnava and Shaiva legends. |
| 15 | **Kūrma Purāṇa** | 17,000 | Tamas | Shiva / Vishnu | Kurma avatar of Vishnu; synthesis of Vishnu- and Shiva-related legends, pilgrimage (Tirtha), and theology. |
| 16 | **Matsya Purāṇa** | 14,000 | Tamas | Shiva / Vishnu | Story of the Matsya avatar, geography of the Narmada river basin, Brahma-Saraswati legends, and royal genealogies. |
| 17 | **Garuḍa Purāṇa** | 19,000 | Sattva | Vishnu | Encyclopedic dialogue between Vishnu and Garuda; karma, rebirth, afterlife, medicine, gemology, and Samkhya/Advaita Yoga. |
| 18 | **Brahmāṇḍa Purāṇa** | 12,000 | Rajas | Brahma | One of the earliest Puranas; contains the *Lalitā Sahasranāmam*, dynastic genealogies, law codes, and statecraft. |

### Alternate Texts Counted in Mahāpurāṇa Traditions

*   **Vāyu Purāṇa**: Often substituted for or equated with the Shiva Purana in canonical lists; shares extensive parallel material with the Brahma, Vishnu, and Markandeya Puranas.
*   **Devī-Bhāgavata Purāṇa**: Admitted as a Mahāpurāṇa by the *Vāyu Purāṇa*, *Matsya Purāṇa*, and *Āditya Upa-Purāṇa*, while regarded as an Upapurāṇa in other traditions.

# Ati-purāṇas (Sthala, Kula, and Minor Upapuranas)

These texts comprise regional chronicles, caste or community records (Kula Puranas), pilgrimage manuals (Sthala Puranas), and specialized sectarian works:

| S.No. | Text | Tradition / Classification | Primary Subject Matter |

| 1 | **Bṛihaddharma Purāṇa** | Non-sectarian | Late canonical compilation on dharma, social conduct, and mythology. |
| 2 | **Kālki Purāṇa** | Vaishnava | Eschatology and the arrival of the Kalki avatar of Vishnu. |
| 3 | **Śivarahasya Purāṇa** | Shaiva | Esoteric Shaiva worship, philosophy, and legends. |
| 4 | **Kriyāyogasāra Purāṇa** | Vaishnava | Practical devotional rituals, pilgrimage, and Bhakti practices. |
| 5 | **Yuga Purāṇa** | Astrological / Historical | Historical, astronomical, and cyclical epochal narratives. |
| 6 | **Lakṣmī Purāṇa** | Shakta / Vaishnava | Worship and social themes surrounding Goddess Lakshmi. |
| 7 | **Bhaviṣyottara Purāṇa** | Non-sectarian | Supplementary material, rituals, and fasts linked to the Bhavishya corpus. |
| 8 | **Devāṅga Purāṇa** | Kula (Caste) | Traditional origin, lineage, and rituals of the Devanga weaving community. |
| 9 | **Nīlamata Purāṇa** | Sthala / Regional | Sacred geography, mythology, rites, and early history of Kashmir. |

| 17 | **Ṛju Purāṇa** | Regional / Minor | Traditional mythological compilation. |
