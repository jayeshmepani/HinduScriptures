## Upāṅga

The four Upāṅgas. With the four Vedas and the six Vedāṅgas, these are the fourteen Vidyāsthānas. Ṣaḍdarśana is a separate scheme of six philosophical systems. The two schemes overlap in subject, and a work already catalogued is not copied into both.

### Purāṇa

Itihāsa is under Rāmāyaṇa and Mahābhārata. The Mahāpurāṇas, Upapurāṇas, and Atipurāṇas stay in those sections.

### Nyāya

Nyāya and Vaiśeṣika are under Ṣaḍdarśana.

### Mīmāṃsā

Pūrvamīmāṃsā and Uttaramīmāṃsā are under Ṣaḍdarśana.

### Dharmaśāstra

The Dharmasūtras are under Vedāṅga Kalpa. The Smṛtis, Upasmṛtis, and Dharma Nibandhas stay in those sections.
