### Key Consolidation & Disambiguation Notes:
1. **Identically Named Works Disambiguated**: Where multiple distinct texts share the same title, they are indexed with distinct numerical suffixes and their exact origin texts and speaker–listener dialogues (e.g., **Śrī Rāma Gītā 1–4**, **Yama Gītā 1–3**, **Brahma Gītā 1–3**, **Haṁsa Gītā 1–2**, **Kapila Gītā 1–2**, **Rudra Gītā 1–2**, **Sūrya Gītā 1–2**, **Vāsiṣṭha Gītā 1–2**, **Mokṣa Gītā 1–2**).
2. **Śiva Gītā vs. Īśvara Gītā**: These are **two distinct texts**. *Śiva Gītā* belongs to the *Padma Purāṇa* (Lord Śiva instructing Śrī Rāma), whereas *Īśvara Gītā* belongs to the *Kūrma Purāṇa* (Uttara-vibhāga Ch. 1–11, Lord Śiva instructing sages and deities).
3. **Īśvara Gītā vs. Vyāsa Gītā**: In the *Kūrma Purāṇa*, *Īśvara Gītā* forms Chapters 1–11 of the Uttaravibhāga, while *Vyāsa Gītā* directly follows it as Chapters 12–42.
4. **All Prose Additions Integrated**: Works mentioned in the prose notes (*Uddhava Gītā*, *Vibhīṣaṇa Gītā*, *Bodhya Gītā*, *Brāhmaṇa Gītā*, *Haṁsa Gītā [Purāṇic]*, *Kapila Gītā 2*, *Yama Gītā 3*) are fully integrated into their respective scriptural sections.
5. **Anthology Recensions Kept Intact**: The *Pañcādaśa Gītā* collection is preserved in its own section, showing how those 9 texts were curated from the *Mahābhārata*.
